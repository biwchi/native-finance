# Brand icon sources

Finance Tracker uses these marks only to let people identify their own accounts, subscriptions, merchants, and asset categories. Their inclusion does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

## Bundled Simple Icons artwork

The SVG files came from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) revision [`b86d5c9a0bdd4f3f5c30898a63654dd32f39fd76`](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/b86d5c9a0bdd4f3f5c30898a63654dd32f39fd76). They are stored as monochrome template images so the existing picker can apply its selected, disabled, light, and dark appearance.

Simple Icons releases its project under CC0, but its [legal disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md) makes clear that individual marks can remain protected by copyright or trademark rules. Check the linked brand guidelines again before release because owners can change them.

## Bybit

`brand-bybit` is derived from the official wordmark published on the [Bybit press page](https://www.bybit.com/press). The press preview was cropped to the wordmark, its white background was removed, and the result is rendered as a monochrome template image. Bybit's trademark remains subject to its terms; source availability is not a general trademark license.

## Excluded after permission review

The app does not bundle the following requested marks:

- No public license was found that permits the Kaspi or Ozon logos in an unrelated app. Their official sites provide product and merchant materials, not general app-use permission.
- Wildberries says its logo requires consent in its [official advertising requirements](https://cmp.wildberries.ru/cmpf/%D0%A2%D0%B5%D1%85%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B5%20%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B1%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8F%20%D0%BA%20%D0%BE%D1%84%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BC%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8E%20%D0%B1%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%BD%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2.pdf).
- Freedom Bank requires written consent for logo and trademark use in its [partner rules](https://support.bankffin.kz/dlya-partnerov/kakie-pravila-i-ogranicheniya-dejstvuyut-dlya-agenta-partnera).
- Russian bank marks are excluded until each owner grants written permission. Sber's [developer rules](https://developers.sber.ru/docs/ru/va/about/legal-entities/legal-requirements) require proof of ownership or permission for third-party branding.

## USDC

`crypto-usdc` is the monochrome `USDC-symbol.png` from Circle's official [USDC logo package](https://www.circle.com/pressroom). The filename changed when it was added to the asset catalog; the artwork did not. Circle permits the symbol in monochrome settings and publishes separate rules for its colored token logo, clear space, and minimum size.
